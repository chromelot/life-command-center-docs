# Chrome Lot — Sales

New business pipeline. Pipedrive Pipeline 1. Used by weekly meeting Phase 6.

## Goal + priority posture (added 2026-06-02)

Chrome Lot's job right now is to **protect revenue + replace churn + pursue moderate growth** — not to maximize new business. Chrome Lot is often *urgent but not always important*; **new-business / aggressive growth is deprioritized below Turbo Gear** (the strategic priority) and yields first when capacity is tight. Size sales activity to the churn-replacement goal; surplus energy beyond the revenue floor goes to TG. See [`../../self/current-priorities.md`](../../self/current-priorities.md) priority stack.

## Pipeline structure

- **Pipedrive Pipeline 1**: Sales deals (prospects being sold to)
- **Stages**: Aware, Warm, Hot (and others)

## Weekly sales workflow (Phase 6 of weekly meeting)

### Part A — Aaron's sales activity

1. Review Pipeline 1: new leads, stale deals, overdue activities
2. Plan Aaron's sales stops for the week (realistically **2-3 stops**)
3. **Route planning**: Aaron uses Mapsly manually to cluster stops geographically
4. Review any rescheduled-3x activities — decide: do, delegate, or drop
5. Accountability: activities completed last week vs. planned

### Part B — Team sales oversight

1. Pull Pipeline 1 deals owned by Tristen, Lexie, Ran
2. Review each team member's: deals in progress, overdue activities, stale leads
3. Flag any deals with **no activity in 14+ days**
4. Propose reassignments or follow-up nudges
5. Create Pipedrive activities for team members as needed

## Cap

- **Max 3 Pipedrive activities/day** (cross-cutting rule). If suggesting more for any single day, cap at 3.
- **Max 2-3 sales stops/week for Aaron** specifically. Sales stops are slower than CS stops because they often turn into discovery conversations.
