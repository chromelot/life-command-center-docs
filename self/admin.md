# Admin — operational rules

Picture-of-success summary in [values.md](values.md). This file is the operational layer for admin / householder discipline.

## Time target

**Regular, organized efforts.** From Values DB.

## Sanctified householder

The framing for this category. Aaron is a **sanctified householder**:

> I see to practical needs — parenting, work, resource management, professional relationships, and personal administration. I approach these with the warrior's discipline, the magician's intelligence, and shamanic wisdom. The last is the most important — it keeps the work done with love while keeping my soul from becoming entangled.

The goal is **handling life without becoming life**. Don't let admin grow beyond its proportion.

## Operating rules

### Keep your life simple

Simple ≠ small. As life grows:
- Spend steady time **delegating** (see [`../people/index.md`](../people/index.md) and [`../systems/capacity-rules.md`](../systems/capacity-rules.md) delegation framework)
- Build **systems** to keep things manageable (n8n, Process Street, this Cursor workspace itself)
- Push work down the stack (Tier 1 before Tier 2)

If admin is consuming too much of your week, **something is broken** — either you're holding work that should be delegated, or a system that should exist doesn't.

### Stay grounded

Keep on track with spiritual pursuits (see [spiritual.md](spiritual.md)) so admin doesn't pull you into pure transactional thinking.

### Stay consistent

The schedule and the systems work because they're consistent:
- Weekly meeting (60 min, scheduled)
- Monthly plan (70 min)
- Quarterly plan (90-120 min)
- Micro-scrubs (3x/week, 10 min each)
- Inbox triage (biweekly, 10 min)

Skipping cadences erodes the system. The weekly meeting workflow surfaces missed cadences with specific recommendations.

## Why this matters

> Live as a responsible adult in the world without becoming overcome by banal concerns. You set the conditions for being a good parent, pursuing spiritual truth, and more.

Admin discipline is **infrastructure** for the other categories. Underdoing it costs everything. Overdoing it traps you.
